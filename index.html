FEMME by VISTORIA opens with a shock of saffron and bergamot that refuses to whisper. At its heart, Bulgarian rose and oud resin collide in an accord that belongs to neither East nor West. The base lingers — agarwood, amber, and white musk — like a conversation you can’t walk away from.

Born between the souks of Arabia and the ateliers of Florence.

It does not introduce. It announces.
